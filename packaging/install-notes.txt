OSINT Digest for Windows

Send publisher research summaries to your own Discord channel.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), internet access, and a Discord channel webhook.
Python is included. Gemini summaries require your own Gemini API key. API usage follows your provider's quota and billing.

After installation, open Connection to enter your webhook and key, then Preview batch before sending.

Automatic checks run while the app is open and the PC is awake. Closing the app stops checking.
Settings and delivery history are saved under your Windows account. Uninstalling the app preserves that data.
